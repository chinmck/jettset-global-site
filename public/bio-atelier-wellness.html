Jettset Aviation Wellness

Aviation Wellness

The Journey, Reimagined.

Jettset Aviation Wellness is a new approach to supporting how travellers prepare, perform and recover before, during and after every journey.

Bringing together recovery technology, curated supplementation, selected wellness treatments and specialist partners, the experience is built around the individual, the journey and what comes next.

Explore Aviation Wellness

Time in the air doesn’t have to be lost time.

Long journeys, demanding schedules, changing time zones and the pace of modern life can place very different demands on the body.

Aviation Wellness has been created around a simple idea: the journey itself can become part of how you prepare, reset and arrive.

From personalised supplementation and recovery support to selected wellness treatments and technologies, each experience can be considered around the traveller, the journey and what comes next.

Arrive Ready.
01

Prepare

Before departure, wellness can be considered around the journey ahead — from hydration and supplementation to selected treatments designed around individual priorities.

02

Reset

In the air, the focus shifts to comfort, recovery and intelligent use of time — with selected supplementation, recovery technologies and wellness rituals incorporated into the journey where appropriate.

03

Arrive

The objective is simple: to arrive feeling more considered, more prepared and better placed for whatever is waiting on the other side.
